🧠 How Fuzzy Matching Works

Our recommendation engine uses Fuzzy Logic (scikit-fuzzy) to evaluate shipments. Three input factors are analyzed — Capacity Fit (how well available space matches your cargo), Cost Efficiency (relative pricing), and Urgency (timeline match). The engine applies human-readable rules (e.g., "good capacity + cheap = excellent match") to produce an explainable score from 0 to 100.
